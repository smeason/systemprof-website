CapstoneOct 2025 – Dec 2025

For my capstone, I worked in a team in the health industry to develop a contactless system that estimates biomarkers, including blood pressure, blood oxygen saturation, and BMI from facial video. We researched, gathered data, and evaluated different ML models on this task. I did the blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) prediction using a method which converts facial regions to spatio-temporal maps, separates AC and DC signals, and uses a dual-stream ResNet model for estimation. The idea was to use the steady-state and periodic signals to find insights into the blood flow and in turn the blood oxygen. Paper (pdf) Code

Generating Animal Hybrids Using Adversarial AutoencodersFeb 2024 – Apr 2024

Developed and evaluated deep learning models for generating realistic animal hybrids through latent-space image interpolation. Using the AFHQ animal-face dataset, we reproduced ACAI and implemented an improved AEAI architecture combining autoencoders with adversarial training, LPIPS reconstruction, and cycle-consistency losses. AEAI consistently produced sharper, more realistic hybrids than ACAI, achieving substantially lower FID scores on both training and validation data. Training was difficult due to instability of the actor-critic architecture, often resulting in mode collapse. This was mitigated with weight clipping. Paper (pdf) Preliminary Report (pdf) Code

Rubik's Cube Solving Robot2013

Built in 2013 following a tutorial. It solves a rubik's cube in less than 2 minutes using the left arm to flip the cube or hold it in place, and the bottom turntable to make the moves. The rubik's cube is scanned using an rbg sensor on the right arm to then calculate for the optimal moves.
