Robotics

FLL 2014 (Falconators)

I was a leader in the robotics department for the 2014 FLL competition. There were many different tasks which needed different types of arms and motor setups to accomplish. After each mini-run, we would switch out different arms/attachments for the next run. The preperation involved intensive testing and adjustments to program the robot to complete the missions as reliably as possible.

Our team recieved the Research award.

Silver Stream Robotics

Captain of the Silver Stream Robotics Team achieving 2nd in the York Region Competition in 2016. The robot was made using Lego EV3 and was designed to put balls into boxes with the help of a line follower.

FLL 2015 (Metal Talons)

I also led my team in the robotics department for the 2015 competition. It was a similar process to the 2014 competition, but we implemented new strategies we learned from the previous year.

U of T Summer Camp

Led my group in the "Design, Build, Battle" summer DEEP Program at U of T (2019). The goal was to make a robot to pick up and transport wooden cubes.

The robot is constructed with acrylic, cardboard and 3d printed parts. It is controlled via bluetooth with the Arduino bluetooth controller app.

Rubik's Cube Solving Robot

Built in 2013 following a tutorial. It solves a rubik's cube in less than 2 minutes using the left arm to flip the cube or hold it in place, and the bottom turntable to make the moves. The rubik's cube is scanned using an rbg sensor on the right arm to then calculate for the optimal moves.
